⚖️ Farmer Rights & Agricultural Acts (किसान अधिकार)

Simplified, icon-based legal rights every Indian farmer should know.

🏪

Right to Sell Anywhere (Freedom of Trade)

You are legally free to sell your produce directly to consumers, FPOs, food processors, or bulk buyers outside the traditional APMC mandi without paying mandatory agent commission fees.

💰

MSP Procurement Guarantee

The Government fixes Minimum Support Prices (MSP) for 22 notified crops. Government procurement centers must purchase notified crops at or above MSP during procurement season.

📜

Digital 7/12 Record & Subsidy Protection

Your digital Mahabhulekh (7/12 extract) is your proof of agricultural land. No private buyer or trader can claim rights or mortgage over your farmland during trade transactions.

🛡️

72-Hour Crop Loss Claim Right

In case of localized storm or flood damage, you have the right to report crop loss to PMFBY within 72 hours via the Crop Insurance App or Toll-Free number for 100% claim assessment.
