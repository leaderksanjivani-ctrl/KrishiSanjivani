MahaDBT Maharashtra Subsidy Portal Guide

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) for tractors, drip irrigation, and seed subsidies

Key MahaDBT Agriculture Schemes Available:

  • 🚜 Mechanization Scheme: 50% subsidy (up to ₹1.25 Lakh) on Tractors & Rotavators.
  • 💧 Drip & Sprinkler Irrigation (POCRA): Up to 80% subsidy for small farmers.
  • 🌱 Certified Seed Distribution: Direct subsidy credited to Bank Aadhaar account.
  • ⚡ Solar Agriculture Pump (PM-KUSUM): 90% total combined subsidy.
